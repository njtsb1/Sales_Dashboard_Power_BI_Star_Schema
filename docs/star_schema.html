Star Schema — Focus: Professor

Dimensional model centered on professor analytics. The fact table captures teaching and performance measures; dimensions provide descriptive context.

FactProfessorTeaching

  • FactID (surrogate PK)
  • ProfessorID (FK)
  • CourseID (FK)
  • DisciplineID (FK)
  • DepartmentID (FK)
  • DateID (FK)
  • TeachingHours (measure)
  • CoursesTaughtCount (measure)
  • AvgCourseEnrollment (measure)
  • PublicationsCount (measure)
  • ResearchGrantsAmount (measure)
  • Salary (measure)
  • TenureStatus (attribute)

DimProfessor

  • ProfessorID (PK)
  • FullName
  • HireDate
  • Rank
  • Title
  • Gender
  • BirthDate
  • Email
  • DepartmentID (FK)

DimDepartment

  • DepartmentID (PK)
  • DepartmentName
  • Campus
  • CoordinatorProfessorID (FK)

DimCourse

  • CourseID (PK)
  • CourseName
  • Level
  • Credits
  • DepartmentID (FK)

DimDiscipline

  • DisciplineID (PK)
  • DisciplineName
  • SemesterOffered
  • Credits

DimResearch

  • GrantID (PK)
  • GrantAmount
  • GrantStartDate
  • GrantEndDate

DimDate

  • DateID (PK)
  • Date
  • Day
  • Month
  • Quarter
  • Year
  • Semester
  • AcademicYear
  • CourseOfferingDate
  • CourseEndDate

Design & UI Guidelines Implemented

  • Positioning — Golden ratio inspired layout: content column ~61.8% and sidebar ~38.2% for desktop.
  • Contrast — Dark mode default with high contrast text; light mode available.
  • Proportion — Cards and typography sized for visual hierarchy.
  • Segmentation — Clear separation of fact and dimensions for drill-down UX.
  • Navigation — Buttons and menus on every page; keyboard accessible and ARIA labeled.
